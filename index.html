Who we are

We are @thehouseofslocabaia, a multidisciplinary creative platform that started as a fashion label and is now a house of creativity, culture and community.

We create spaces where creativity and business come together through fashion, art, music and community driven projects.

We believe creativity should be more than a form of expression. It should also be a pathway to opportunity, growth and a successful life.
